## 🏛️ Editorial & Design Philosophy

- **Name:** **ICSE Class 7 Science & Mathematics**
- **Layout & Viewport:** Above-the-fold hero with instant discipline tabs, clean search, integrated worked problem spotlight, and bottom utility dock.
- **Typography:** 
  - **Headings & Display:** `Roboto Slab` (optical-weight slab serif for academic dignity without stuffiness).
  - **Body & Data:** `Atkinson Hyperlegible` (high-contrast, legibility-first grotesque sans-serif).
  - **Diagrams & Schematics:** `Abel` (crisp architectural sans-serif for SVG callouts and technical labels).
  - Local high-speed `.woff2` font fallbacks bundled in `fonts/fonts.css`.
- **Palette:** Soft Warm Ivory (`#fcfbf9`) backgrounds, Warm Parchment (`#f7f5f0`) surfaces, Clean Card (`#ffffff`), Deep Carbon (`#121214`) ink, and Muted Terracotta (`#c2410c`) accents.
- **Pedagogical Standard:** The **5:5 Balance** — equal parts physical intuition (anchors in daily life) and mathematical rigor (uncompromised step-by-step derivations, KaTeX formulas, and worked board problems).
- **Tone:** Calm, academic, respectful of the learner. No cartoon gamification or emojis. Includes the official CISCE Non-Standard **Chilling Plan** with an interactive 15-minute mental defragmentation timer.
