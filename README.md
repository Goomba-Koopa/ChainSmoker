# AXIOM ICSE 7 — The Visual Benchmark for CISCE Middle School Excellence

**AXIOM ICSE 7** is a modern, white/light-canvas interactive EdTech learning environment custom-engineered for Class 7 students following the CISCE (Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations) curriculum.

---

## 🏛️ Brand Philosophy & Core Identity

- **Name:** **AXIOM ICSE 7**
- **Tagline:** *The Visual Standard for ICSE Excellence*
- **Design Aesthetic:** High-contrast pure white canvas (`#ffffff`), slate-50/100 panels, deep navy typography (`#0f172a`), 1px crisp borders (`#e2e8f0`), and subtle ambient elevation.
- **Academic Focus:** Zero gamification, zero XP/points, distraction-free rigor, and strict compliance with CISCE examination evaluation formats.

---

## 🔬 Proprietary Interactive Toolsets

### 1. 📝 ICSE "Give Reasons & Distinctions" Examiner Studio
- Formal model answer generator matching the exact marking guidelines of ICSE board evaluators.
- **Give Scientific Reasons:** Underlying causal principles with crucial keyword emphasis.
- **Tabular Distinctions:** 2-column & 3-column comparative criteria tables (e.g. *Conduction vs Convection vs Radiation*, *Mass vs Weight*, *Xylem vs Phloem*, *Fundamental Rights vs Directive Principles*).

### 2. 🧪 Chemical Criss-Cross Valency & Formula Lab
- Interactive basic radicals (monovalent, divalent, trivalent cations: $Na^+, K^+, Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+}, Al^{3+}, Fe^{2+}, Fe^{3+}, NH_4^+$) and acid radicals ($Cl^-, OH^-, NO_3^-, O^{2-}, SO_4^{2-}, CO_3^{2-}, PO_4^{3-}$).
- Visual criss-cross vector animation, common factor reduction, and step-by-step ICSE formula derivation breakdown.

### 3. 📐 Step-by-Step Formula & Dimensional Analysis Studio
- Interactive variable manipulation with live calculation and dimensional cancellation for:
  - Density: $d = m / V$
  - Speed & Velocity: $v = s / t$
  - Simple Pendulum Time Period: $T = t / n$
  - Temperature Scales: $\frac{C}{5} = \frac{F - 32}{9}$
  - Reflection in Inclined Mirrors: $n = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1$

### 4. ⚡ Canonical Textbook Schematics Studio
- Interactive SVG diagrams with interactive hotspots:
  - **Vacuum (Thermos) Flask:** Conduction, convection, and radiation prevention mechanisms.
  - **Open U-Tube Liquid Manometer:** Hydrostatic datum balance and gauge pressure ($\Delta h = h_2 - h_1$).
  - **Bohr Atomic Shells (Elements 1–20):** $K, L, M, N$ electron configuration generator following the $2n^2$ rule.
  - **Leaf Transverse Section (T.S.):** Palisade/spongy mesophyll, vascular bundles (xylem/phloem), and stomatal guard cells.
  - **Laws of Reflection of Light:** Normal line, incident ray, reflected ray, and $\angle i = \angle r$.

### 5. 🔬 Virtual Science Laboratories
- **Light Reflection Sandbox:** Live incidence angle slider ($0^\circ - 85^\circ$), ray propagation, and protractor overlay.
- **Hydrostatic Pressure Manometer:** Gas container pressure controls ($95 - 115\text{ kPa}$) with water vs. mercury density switcher.
- **Dynamic Bohr Atom Generator:** 20-element interactive periodic table selector with valence orbits.
- **Acid-Base Indicator Beaker:** Real-time color transition lab across Litmus, Phenolphthalein, Methyl Orange, and Universal Indicator.
