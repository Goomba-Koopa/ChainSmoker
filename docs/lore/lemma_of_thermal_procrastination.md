# THE LEMMA OF THERMAL PROCRASTINATION

### Theorem:
The rate of cooling of a hot cup of coffee (5 parts coffee, 5 parts milk) is inversely proportional to the user's distance from passing a compiler check, but directly proportional to the number of unescaped quotes in a PowerShell command.

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = -k (T - T_{\text{room}}) \cdot e^{\mu_{\text{regex}}}$$

Where:
- $T$ = Current coffee temperature in °C.
- $T_{\text{room}}$ = Room ambient temperature in the city.
- $\mu_{\text{regex}}$ = Number of attempted inline Python one-liners before giving up and writing a `.py` file.
- $k$ = Universal Constant of Late Night Debugging ($k \approx 4.2 \times 10^3 \text{ s}^{-1}$).

### Corollary:
Coffee reaches thermal equilibrium with the desk precisely three seconds before the user shouts *"Oh, it was a typo in line 42."*
