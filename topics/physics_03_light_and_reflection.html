Physics • Topic 03

Light, Shadows & Reflection at Plane Surfaces

Rectilinear propagation of light, the formal Laws of Reflection (\(\angle i = \angle r\)), Real vs Virtual images, Lateral inversion, and the number of images formed by inclined plane mirrors.

1. The Formal Laws of Reflection

First & Second Laws of Reflection: • First Law: The incident ray, the reflected ray, and the normal to the reflecting surface at the point of incidence, all lie in the same plane.

• Second Law: The angle of incidence (\(\angle i\)) is always equal to the angle of reflection (\(\angle r\)): $$\angle i = \angle r$$
• Normal Incidence Rule: When a light ray strikes a plane mirror perpendicularly (\(\angle i = 0^\circ\)), it reflects directly back along the same path (\(\angle r = 0^\circ\)).

2. Real Image vs. Virtual Image

Characteristic Real Image Virtual Image
Formation Formed when reflected light rays actually intersect at a point. Formed when reflected light rays appear to diverge from behind the mirror.
Screen Capture Can be captured on a physical projection screen. Cannot be captured on a physical screen.
Orientation Always inverted (upside-down) relative to the object. Always erect (upright) relative to the object.
Plane Mirror Example Cinema projector screen, camera sensor. Image seen inside a standard plane dressing table mirror.

3. Number of Images in Inclined Mirrors

Mathematical Formula for Inclined Mirrors at Angle \(\theta\)
Let two plane mirrors be inclined at an angle \(\theta\). First evaluate the quotient: $$Q = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta}$$
• Rule 1 (Even Integer): If \(\frac{360^\circ}{\theta}\) is an even number, the total number of images \(n\) formed is: $$n = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1 \quad \text{(for all positions of object)}$$
• Rule 2 (Odd Integer): If \(\frac{360^\circ}{\theta}\) is an odd number:
    1. Placed symmetrically on the angle bisector: \(n = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta} - 1\)
    2. Placed asymmetrically: \(n = \frac{360^\circ}{\theta}\)

• Parallel Mirrors (\(\theta = 0^\circ\)): An infinite number of images (\(n = \infty\)) are formed.

4. Worked CISCE Exemplar Numericals

Exemplar 3.1: Inclined Mirrors Image Count [3 Marks]
Calculate the number of images formed by two plane mirrors inclined at an angle of: (i) \(60^\circ\), (ii) \(90^\circ\), and (iii) \(40^\circ\).
Step-by-Step Examiner Solution:

1. Case (i) \(\theta = 60^\circ\):
    \(\frac{360^\circ}{60^\circ} = 6\) (Even integer)
    $$n = 6 - 1 = \mathbf{5\text{ images}}$$ [1 Mark]

2. Case (ii) \(\theta = 90^\circ\):
    \(\frac{360^\circ}{90^\circ} = 4\) (Even integer)
    $$n = 4 - 1 = \mathbf{3\text{ images}}$$ [1 Mark]

3. Case (iii) \(\theta = 40^\circ\):
    \(\frac{360^\circ}{40^\circ} = 9\) (Odd integer; symmetrically placed)
    $$n = 9 - 1 = \mathbf{8\text{ images}}$$ [1 Mark]
