Biology • Topic 06

Human Respiratory System & Gas Exchange

The dual mechanics of breathing (Inhalation vs Exhalation), microscopic alveolar diffusion, and Aerobic (\(38\text{ ATP}\)) vs Anaerobic (\(2\text{ ATP}\)) cellular energetics.

1. Inhalation (Inspiration) vs. Exhalation (Expiration)

Mechanical Event Inhalation (Inspiration) Exhalation (Expiration)
Diaphragm Movement Contracts and flattens downwards. Relaxes and arches upwards into dome shape.
External Intercostal Muscles Contract, pulling ribs upwards and outwards. Relax, allowing rib cage to return downwards and inwards.
Thoracic Volume Increases in both dimensions. Decreases to resting baseline.
Intrapulmonary Pressure Drops below atmospheric pressure (\(P_{\text{pulm}} < P_{\text{atm}}\)). Rises above atmospheric pressure (\(P_{\text{pulm}} > P_{\text{atm}}\)).
Direction of Air Flow Atmospheric air rushes into lungs. Alveolar air is expelled out of lungs.

2. Aerobic vs. Anaerobic Cellular Respiration

Comparative Chemical Energetics
• 1. Aerobic Respiration (In Presence of \(\text{O}_2\)):
$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{mitochondria}} 6\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} + \mathbf{38\text{ ATP (2830 kJ)}}$$
• 2. Anaerobic Respiration (In Yeast / Alcoholic Fermentation):
$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 \xrightarrow{\text{zymase}} 2\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH (Ethanol)} + 2\text{CO}_2 + \mathbf{2\text{ ATP (150 kJ)}}$$
• 3. Anaerobic Respiration in Fatigued Human Muscles:
$$\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 \longrightarrow 2\text{C}_3\text{H}_6\text{O}_3\text{ (Lactic Acid)} + \mathbf{2\text{ ATP}} \quad \text{[Causes Muscle Cramps]}$$
