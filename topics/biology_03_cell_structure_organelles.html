Biology • Topic 03

Cell Structure, Cytology & Organelles

The structural and functional unit of life, cell theory, organelle specialization (Mitochondria, Ribosomes, Lysosomes, Plastids), and the 6-point comparative matrix between Plant and Animal cells.

1. The Cell Theory & Microscopic Dimensions

Cell Theory (Schleiden, Schwann & Virchow): 1. All living organisms are composed of one or more microscopic cells.
2. The cell is the fundamental structural and functional unit of life.
3. All cells arise strictly from pre-existing living cells ("Omnis cellula-e-cellula").

• Microscopic Units: \(1\text{ micrometre } (\mu\text{m}) = 10^{-6}\text{ m} = 10^{-3}\text{ mm}\); \(1\text{ Angstrom } (\text{Å}) = 10^{-10}\text{ m}\).

2. Plant Cell vs. Animal Cell

Cytological Feature Plant Cell Animal Cell
Cell Wall Present; rigid outer layer made of cellulose. Absent; enclosed only by thin, flexible cell membrane.
Plastids & Chloroplasts Present (Chloroplasts, Chromoplasts, Leucoplasts). Absent completely.
Vacuole Arrangement One or two large, central, permanent vacuoles with tonoplast. Vacuoles are small, multiple, and temporary.
Centrosome & Centrioles Absent (except in lower algae/fungi). Present; coordinates spindle during cell division.
Cytoplasm Density Pushed to the periphery as a thin lining by central vacuole. Dense, granular cytoplasm fills the entire cell interior.

3. Organelle Roles & Energetics

Key Sub-Cellular Organelles: • Mitochondria ("Powerhouse of the Cell"): Double-membraned organelle with folded inner cristae; site of aerobic cellular respiration generating adenosine triphosphate: $$\text{Energy Yield} = \mathbf{38\text{ ATP molecules per glucose molecule}}$$ • Ribosomes ("Protein Factories"): Granular structures composed of RNA and protein responsible for polypeptide synthesis.
• Lysosomes ("Suicide Bags"): Membrane-bound vesicles packed with \(\approx 40\) powerful digestive hydrolytic enzymes for autolysis of worn-out cellular debris.
