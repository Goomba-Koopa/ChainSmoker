1.0 Master Distinction: Artery vs Vein vs Capillary

Criterion Artery Vein Blood Capillary
1. Direction of Blood Flow Away from heart to body tissues. From tissues towards the heart. Connects arterioles to venules across tissues.
2. Vessel Wall Structure Thick, highly muscular and elastic. Thin, less muscular and non-elastic. Extremely thin single layer of squamous endothelium.
3. Internal Valves Valves are completely absent. Semilunar valves present (prevent backflow). Valves are absent.
4. Lumen Size & Pressure Narrow lumen; blood flows with high pressure and spurts. Wide lumen; blood flows smoothly at low pressure. Microscopic lumen; single-file RBC passage.
