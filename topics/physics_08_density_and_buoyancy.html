Physics • Topic 08

Density, Relative Density & Buoyancy

Volumetric mass packing (\(d = m/V\)), conversion proof (\(1\text{ g/cm}^3 = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3\)), Archimedes' Principle of buoyant upthrust, and the Law of Floatation.

1. Volumetric Density & Unit Conversion

Definition of Density (\(d\) or \(\rho\)): Density is defined as the mass per unit volume of a substance: $$d = \frac{\text{Mass } (m)}{\text{Volume } (V)}$$
• SI Unit: \(\text{kg/m}^3\) (kilogram per cubic metre).
• CGS Unit: \(\text{g/cm}^3\) (gram per cubic centimetre).
Derivation of SI to CGS Density Equivalence
$$1\text{ g/cm}^3 = \frac{1\text{ g}}{1\text{ cm}^3} = \frac{10^{-3}\text{ kg}}{(10^{-2}\text{ m})^3} = \frac{10^{-3}\text{ kg}}{10^{-6}\text{ m}^3} = \mathbf{1,000\text{ kg/m}^3}$$
• Density of Pure Water at \(4^\circ\text{C}\): $$\rho_{\text{water}} = 1.0\text{ g/cm}^3 = 1,000\text{ kg/m}^3$$

2. Relative Density (Specific Gravity)

Relative Density (RD): The ratio of the density of a substance to the density of pure water at \(4^\circ\text{C}\): $$RD = \frac{\text{Density of substance}}{\text{Density of water at } 4^\circ\text{C}} = \frac{\text{Mass of any volume } V \text{ of substance}}{\text{Mass of an equal volume } V \text{ of water at } 4^\circ\text{C}}$$
• Crucial Unit Rule: Since Relative Density is a pure ratio of two identical physical quantities, it has no units (dimensionless).

3. Archimedes' Principle & Law of Floatation

Archimedes' Principle & Upthrust (\(F_B\))
When a body is partially or completely immersed in a fluid at rest, it experiences an upward buoyant force (upthrust) equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by it: $$F_B = V_{\text{sub}} \times \rho_{\text{liquid}} \times g$$
• Law of Floatation: A floating body displaces a volume of liquid whose weight is equal to the total weight of the floating body: $$\text{Weight of floating body} = \text{Weight of displaced liquid}$$
• Why an Iron Nail Sinks while a Heavy Steel Ship Floats: An iron nail is solid, displacing a small volume of water; its weight exceeds the buoyant upthrust (\(W > F_B\)). A steel ship is hollowed out with a large hull, enclosing vast pockets of air, displacing a massive volume of water such that the upward buoyant force balances the entire weight of the ship (\(F_B = W\)).

4. Worked CISCE Exemplar Numerical

Exemplar 8.1: Relative Density & Mass [3 Marks]
The Relative Density of silver is \(10.8\). Calculate: (i) The density of silver in SI units (\(\text{kg/m}^3\)), (ii) The mass of a solid silver block of volume \(250\text{ cm}^3\).
Step-by-Step Examiner Solution:

1. Density in SI Units:
    \(\rho_{\text{silver}} = RD \times \rho_{\text{water}} = 10.8 \times 1,000\text{ kg/m}^3 = \mathbf{10,800\text{ kg/m}^3}\) [1.5 Marks]

2. Mass of Silver Block:
    In CGS: \(\rho = 10.8\text{ g/cm}^3\), Volume \(V = 250\text{ cm}^3\)
    $$m = \rho \times V = 10.8\text{ g/cm}^3 \times 250\text{ cm}^3 = \mathbf{2,700\text{ grams (or } 2.7\text{ kg)}}\) [1.5 Marks]
