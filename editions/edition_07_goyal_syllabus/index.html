1. Physics Curriculum Structure

6 Units
PHY-701: Physical Quantities

Volume of irregular solids (displacement method), Area estimation via graph grids, Mass vs Weight, Density calculations.

PHY-702: Motion & Speed

Types of motion (rectilinear, circular, periodic, oscillatory), Scalar distance vs Vector displacement, Speed formulas.

PHY-703: Energy & Work

Law of Conservation of Energy, Kinetic vs Potential energy transformations, Simple Machines (Lever classes 1, 2, 3).

PHY-704: Light & Reflection

Laws of Reflection (∠i = ∠r, coplanarity), Plane mirror image characteristics, Regular vs Diffuse reflection, Periscope construction.
