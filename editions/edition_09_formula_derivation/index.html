Derivation 9.1: Conversion Factor Between km/h and m/s Physics Proof

1 km = 1,000 metres (m)

1 hour (h) = 60 minutes = 60 × 60 seconds = 3,600 seconds (s)

Therefore: 1 km/h = (1,000 m) / (3,600 s)

Dividing numerator and denominator by 200:

1 km/h = 5 / 18 m/s ≈ 0.2778 m/s

Conversely: 1 m/s = 18 / 5 km/h = 3.6 km/h

Derivation 9.2: Conversion Factor Between CGS Density (g/cm³) and SI (kg/m³) Dimensional Proof

1 g = 1 / 1,000 kg = 10⁻³ kg

1 cm = 1 / 100 m = 10⁻² m ==> 1 cm³ = (10⁻² m)³ = 10⁻⁶ m³

Therefore: 1 g/cm³ = (10⁻³ kg) / (10⁻⁶ m³) = 10⁻³ × 10⁶ kg/m³

1 g/cm³ = 1,000 kg/m³

Example: Density of water = 1 g/cm³ = 1,000 kg/m³

Example: Density of Mercury = 13.6 g/cm³ = 13,600 kg/m³
