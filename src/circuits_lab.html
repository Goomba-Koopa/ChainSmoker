Magnetic Field Laws:
• Field lines emerge from the North (N) pole and enter into the South (S) pole externally.
• The tangent at any point on a field line gives the direction of the magnetic field vector ($\vec{B}$).
• Lines never intersect each other.
• Density of lines indicates magnetic field strength.
