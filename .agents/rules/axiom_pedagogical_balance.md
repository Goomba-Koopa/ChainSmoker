# The 5:5 "Coffee-to-Milk" Pedagogical Standard

Every STEM topic in AXIOM ICSE 7 must maintain an exact **50% Milk / 50% Coffee** balance:

## 1. Five Parts Milk (Intuition & Breathing Room)
- **Phenomenon-First Opening Hook**: Never begin with dry dictionary definitions. Use relatable physical analogies (e.g. Wankhede Stadium for atom scale, Russian dolls for number continuum, balance beam for linear equations).
- **Plain-English Middle School Tone**: Tailored to a sharp 12-year-old student (`"i am smart, not every 7th grader bro. It is hard to understand."`).
- **Common Student Trap Boxes**: Prominent `⚠️ Common Student Trap` callout boxes pointing out high-frequency exam blunders (e.g. sign distribution traps, bracket omission in polyatomic radicals, division by zero fallacies).

## 2. Five Parts Coffee (Rigor & Mathematical Substance)
- **Full ICSE Coverage**: Zero hand-waving or dumbing down of core principles.
- **KaTeX Mathematical Rigor**: Clean inline `$..$` and block `$$..$$` equations.
- **Multi-Tier Worked Problems**: Minimum of 3 worked problems featuring step-by-step logic and collapsible solution drawers (`.btn-toggle` + `.solution-drawer`).
- **Scientific Reasoning Drawers**: Dedicated "Why?" deduction prompts with interactive reveal toggles.
